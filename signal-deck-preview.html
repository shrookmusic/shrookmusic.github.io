About Shrook

Shrook is a Dubai-based DJ and music producer with 9 years of DJing and 8 years of production experience. His sound blends psychedelic textures, hypnotic rhythms, rolling basslines and driving percussion, drawing from psytrance, underground techno and hard groove.

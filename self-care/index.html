Use this gently

Pick an action, try the smaller version, or skip the card. The Jungian reflection is optional; it is a prompt for curiosity, not a diagnosis or a fixed interpretation.

Your sensory needs and stims are valid information. Masking can be protective; there is no pressure to unmask or explore anything that does not feel safe.
